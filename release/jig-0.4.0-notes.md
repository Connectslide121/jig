## New

- **Manage your Azure DevOps boards in Jig:** open a card to read and edit it (title, description, state, assignee, tags, comments), drag cards between columns, add cards to a column and delete them. Claude can read and update your boards from any chat, so you can keep work items current while you work.
- **Teams chat in meetings and calls:** a Chat panel shows the meeting's or call's Teams chat, with a box to write in it. The chat is kept with the meeting, and Claude's notes include it. Needs your administrator to approve Jig's Chat.Read and ChatMessage.Send permissions.
- **Profile pictures** of the people in your meetings and calls: on their tiles, in People, beside their captions and when calling.
- **A frame around what you share:** the screen or window you share in a meeting gets a coloured frame that only you see.
- **File paths in chats are links:** paths Claude writes open the file or folder in Jig.

## Improved

- Meetings that already took place stay in the calendar, faded, and you can still join them.
- A workflow's pipeline step (and its "after a pipeline" trigger) is picked from a list of your pipelines instead of typed in.
- Generated meeting notes ask to **Save notes and actions for review**, making clear nothing runs until you send actions to the task queue.
- The task queue's progress sits in its header row, and the queue fits a sidebar.
- Board tabs say the team and the board, for example "Shop Team · Stories".
- Open Browser moved into the Chats menu.

## Fixed

- Boards showed their columns but no cards when the Azure DevOps project name had a space in it.
- The meeting transcript could run under the status bar when its panel shared a column with Notes.
