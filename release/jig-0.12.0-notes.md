## New

- **Jig on your phone.** Connect a Telegram bot of your own in Settings → Phone (free, two minutes). While you're away, Jig messages you when a chat needs you, finishes or stops: tap Allow, Deny, an option or Approve the plan, or reply to answer.
- **Talk to Jig itself from your phone.** Ask how things are going, or have it start a chat in a project ("look at card 1234 and make a plan"); its answers come back marked as AI. `/status`, `/queue`, `/pause`, `/resume` and `/screen` answer at once.
- **Chats as rooms.** Open any chat on your phone (its ⋯ menu → Open on my phone, the Open chat button, or just ask) and follow it live: your messages, Claude's replies as they grow, its steps and screenshots. Write there to talk to it.
- **Voice messages.** Send a voice note: Jig transcribes it on your computer and answers in text and read aloud.
- **Screenshots on your phone.** Chats can send their browser page, and finished turns that used the browser come with it.
- **One topic per project.** Turn on topics for your bot in @BotFather and each project gets its own thread, with one for Jig.
- **The phone switch** at the top right turns it on when you leave and off when you're back.
- **Run reports.** In Tasks → History, ask for a report of a finished run: what was done, what needs you and follow-ups you can queue.
- **Mark a failed task as done** by hand, with a note.

## Improved

- The task queue's summary strip adds up: done of what will run, then failed, running and left out, with time and cost.
- History shows which provider each run's tasks ran on.
- A chat can correct the report of a task it ran.
- Drag tasks in the queue and scroll with the mouse wheel while dragging.
- Your computer stays awake while chats work and your phone is connected (Settings → Phone).

## Fixed

- Several rare timing problems in chats and views that only showed on a slow window.
