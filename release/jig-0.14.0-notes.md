## New

- **Teams as your phone.** Settings → Phone now offers Telegram or Teams. Set up Teams walks you through creating a small relay in your own Azure subscription (every command is listed before it runs), adding the Jig app to Teams and pairing. Jig gets a team with a channel per project and a thread per chat, and asks for what it needs in your 1:1 chat with it. Setting it up needs a tenant that lets you upload your own Teams apps.
- **Bottom panel span.** Choose whether the bottom panel sits under the centre only, also under the left or right sidebar, or across the whole window: Settings → Layout or the bottom panel's menu.
- **Limits resume by themselves.** When a chat hits a plan limit and you don't answer the card, it continues on its own at the reset, also after sleep or a restart.
- **Your Teams chat with yourself** shows in Meetings → Chats, where you can read it, write in it and mention it with @chat.
- **More for Claude:** it can read your usage limits for both plans, look at pipelines and repositories (read only, never starting a run) and close a chat's room on your phone.

## Improved

- **Jig never steals focus while you type.** A terminal a chat starts, or another chat becoming active, no longer pulls the caret away from the field you're typing in; an incoming Teams call doesn't take the keyboard either.
- Meeting caption translation keeps up with the conversation: captions are translated in batches, and lines that waited too long stay as spoken and say so.
- Terminals Claude starts close by themselves when the chat is done with them, and a terminal shows a badge instead of switching while you work in its panel.
- The Boards New card dialog is the card's whole form.
- Send and its arrow read as one split button, and the arrow is disabled when Send is.

## Fixed

- Your own Teams captions carry your name instead of Unknown speaker.
- Teams captions no longer stop right after joining when Teams reports the feature too early.
- The phone reconnects to Telegram right after the computer wakes up, and the unreachable warning clears once a message gets through.
- Pasting or dropping empty entries into the chat box no longer adds empty attachments.
