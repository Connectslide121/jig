## New

- **Park a prompt.** The arrow beside Send opens a menu: Send / Queue, Send now, Park and Send in a new chat. Parked prompts keep their text and files, stay with the chat and are never sent by themselves; send, queue or remove them when you're ready.
- **Parked ideas in the Tasks queue.** Keep ideas below a "Parked" divider at the end of the queue: they never run until you unpark them. Park and unpark from a task's menu or by dragging.
- **Don't ask for queued tasks.** A task can now run in Don't ask mode, chosen when you add or edit it.
- **Go to finds settings.** Search for what's on a settings page ("telegram" finds Phone), and actions by their keywords.

## Improved

- **Much less memory.** Chats you aren't using let go of their Claude Code or Codex process after 20 minutes (Settings → Chats → Stop idle chats) and pick up where they left off on your next message. Closed chats' transcripts are unloaded, and long chats render their latest messages first, loading more as you scroll up.
- **Meetings use less memory after a call**, and calls still ring in Jig.
- **Permission mode is back in the message box**, right after the Claude/ChatGPT picker.
- The Tasks queue's header shows fixed counts (done, running, to run, left out, parked) that you can click and that never shift.
- The meeting's live transcript keeps following new lines until you scroll up, with a Latest button to jump back.
- A picture in a meeting chat opens in a viewer that fills the window.
- On your phone, Claude's replies arrive formatted (bold, code, lists) instead of raw Markdown.
- Claude can delete queued tasks (after you approve), and its test runs report back while long suites are still running.

## Fixed

- The Teams status mark sits centred in its dot.
- Claude and Codex edit files with their edit tools instead of shell scripts, which could garble quotes and line breaks.
