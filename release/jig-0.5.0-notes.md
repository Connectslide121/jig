## New

- **"Hey Jig" voice commands.** Turn it on in Settings → Speech → Hey Jig, then say "Hey Jig" followed by a command to open chats, check in on work, run things or control Jig, even while Jig is minimized. Commands run directly on this computer without Claude; deploys and pulls are read back and wait for a spoken yes. Jig can also say when chats finish or need you. The full list is on the new Voice commands page (Jig menu → Voice commands), and a status bar item shows when Jig is listening.
- **Translated meeting captions without Teams Premium.** When Teams can't translate captions into the language you pick, Claude translates each finished caption instead. Translated lines are marked "AI translation, may be inaccurate", with the original kept underneath. You can switch this off or pick the model in the meeting settings.
- **Choose your microphone and speaker.** Settings → Speech → Microphone and speaker, or the headphones button while talking, picks the microphone dictation and talking listen to and the speaker voices play on. An unplugged microphone falls back to the default.
- **More control over the task queue.** Queued tasks have a tick (all ticked by default) and Start skips the unticked ones. Put back in the queue returns a finished, skipped or running task to the queue, and Start over resets every task.

## Improved

- **Talking with Claude ignores background noise.** Only a voice starts, keeps or interrupts a turn now; dishes, typing and coughs no longer cut Claude off or appear as your words. Adjust it in Settings → Speech → Ignore background noise.
- The task queue summary shows how many chats the tasks run in.

## Fixed

- Words from an earlier spoken turn no longer reappear at the start of later messages when you speak while Claude is still answering.
- Notifications bring up Jig's main window instead of the hidden meeting window.
- A selection on the editor's active line is visible again.
