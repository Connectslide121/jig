## New

- **All queues dashboard.** The Tasks view has an **All queues** button that opens one tab with a card per project's queue: what's running, what needs you, progress, time and cost. Click a card to open that queue; start or pause it on hover.
- **Choose Claude or ChatGPT for task queues.** Each queue has a **Runs on** choice in the Tasks header (project default, Claude or ChatGPT), and each task can pick its own provider and model when you add or edit it. Every task shows which provider it runs on.
- **Teams status in Meetings.** People's availability shows as a dot on their picture in one-to-one chats and in a call's People panel. Turn it on from the Chats list's menu.
- **Pictures and shared files in meeting chats.** Images show as thumbnails that open in a viewer, and shared files appear as rows that open in your browser.
- **Add-ons can start chats on Claude or ChatGPT**, with a chosen model.

## Improved

- Meeting chats look like Teams: your messages on the right in bubbles, others on the left with picture and name, grouped messages, day dividers and clickable links. Your reading position stays put when new messages arrive.
- Senders from other organisations are now named in meeting chats instead of showing up blank.
- Claude can find and edit tasks in any project's queue, not only the current one.
- The status bar has a divider between Jig's own items and add-on items.
