## New

- **Carry on when a chat hits its limit.** When your plan's limit stops a chat, a card offers to continue the same chat with the other provider (Claude or ChatGPT), wait and resume at the reset, or dismiss. Choose the default in Settings → Chats or per project; the task queue follows it.
- **Hand a chat over at any time.** Between turns, switch a chat to the other provider from the composer's provider picker or the chat's ⋯ menu. A dialog shows what carries over, and a divider marks the handover in the chat.
- **ChatGPT chats get search and tester helpers**, each on its own model (Settings → Chats → ChatGPT chats).
- **Boards cards like Azure DevOps.** A card now has Details, Links and Attachments tabs, rich text with images, every field, and its linked items, pull requests, commits, branches and builds.
- **Add-ons can ask before Jig quits.** The Time add-on asks while a timer runs; one "Quit Jig?" dialog lists every reason.
- **Pick a new chat's project** in its header before the first message; switching project in the top bar moves the new chat too.

## Improved

- The permission mode now shows as a chip in the chat's header and sits in the model menu.
- The composer's Send, Queue and Stop buttons are icons, with tooltips naming the key.
- Every row in the Chats list shows its provider's mark.
- Files keeps its tree when you switch away and refreshes in the background, so it no longer reloads.
- Claude's browser: one-line tabs with Open chat and Close page buttons on hover, the sign-in hint at the bottom, and a badge instead of taking over the panel you're working in.
- Repos and Pipelines: Arrange is an icon button.

## Fixed

- Saving a Boards card's description no longer removes its images.
- Pipelines no longer shows another tenant's errors after switching tenant during a search.
