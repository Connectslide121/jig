## Improved

- **One status mark for chats:** a chat's tab, the Chats list, its header, the Chats board, Home and Attempts all show the same mark in the project's colour: bouncing dots while it works, a bell when it needs you, an alert on an error, and a bright corner dot when it finished a turn you haven't seen yet.
- **Chat tabs in the project's colour:** the chat icon in a tab is filled with its project's colour, so tabs from different projects are easy to tell apart.
- **Screenshots stay visible:** in the compact view, screenshots and images Jig read now show as small previews under the folded steps line; click one to open it in a tab.

## Fixed

- **Task queue is back:** in 0.17.0 the Task queue view and its tabs were missing from the installed app. They load again.
- **Add-ons that fail to load:** when a switched-on add-on can't load, Jig now says so in a notification with the first error line and a link to Settings, instead of silently hiding its views.
- **Read cards:** a Read card no longer shows a broken thumbnail when the file is gone.
