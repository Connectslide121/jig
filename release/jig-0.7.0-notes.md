## New

- **ChatGPT as a second AI provider.** Sign in to the Codex CLI from Get started, then pick Claude or ChatGPT, and a model, for each of Jig's AI features (commit messages, release notes, chat names, caption translation and more) in **Settings → AI features**. Chats stay on Claude Code.
- **ChatGPT plan usage in the footer**, next to Claude's, with both in the hover card.
- **Meeting lobby in its own window.** Agenda, history, reviews and actions stay usable beside a call.
- **Your Teams chats in the lobby:** read and reply to your newest chats, and open a one-to-one chat from **Call…**.
- **Meeting history in folders:** make your own folders, and meetings of a recurring series are grouped automatically.
- **Boards toolbar** with search and filters for type, assignee, state, tags, iteration and area, kept per board.
- **Assignee avatars** on board cards.
- **Times in chats:** the clock button in a chat's top bar adds a time column; otherwise hover a message to see its time.
- **New chats get a short AI-written name** from their first message (turn it off in Settings → Claude Code).
- **Builds get a clear title**, and the build page shows your request in full.
- **Add-ons** can place items at the start of the top bar's right side and fill top bar menus with live entries.

## Improved

- Boards load at startup and refresh in the background, so tabs open instantly with the last cards.
- Project notes are now part of the project's knowledge, shown on Home next to its documents. Existing notes are moved over automatically.
- Your own az login can be renamed like any other tenant.
- Builder chats start in your default permission mode.
- Chats in **Don't ask** mode no longer stop for Claude Code's own safety prompts; Jig declines them and shows a notice.

## Fixed

- Board cards created from a chat keep characters such as – … and ö, descriptions with quotes no longer fail, and deleting a card works.
- When the builder proposes a new extension point, the add-on it already built is kept.
- The task queue shows its toolbar labels and all its table columns again.
