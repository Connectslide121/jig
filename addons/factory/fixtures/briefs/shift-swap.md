# Shift swap

## Problem
Care homes plan staff shifts weeks ahead, and when someone can't work a shift, the planner phones
around until a colleague takes it. It takes an hour a day and shifts still go uncovered.

## Value
Staff swap shifts among themselves in two taps, within the rules the planner set, and the planner
only approves.

## Users
Care staff (on a phone), the shift planner (on a laptop).

## Main flow
A carer offers a shift → colleagues who may take it get a notice → one accepts → the planner
approves → both rosters update.

## Key screens
My shifts (phone), Offer a shift, Open offers, Planner's approvals, Week roster.

## Scope
The demo shows the main flow with invented staff of Contoso Care, one week.

## Out of scope
Payroll, sick leave, integrations.

## Data
Staff, shifts, offers, approvals. All invented.

## Rules
Only colleagues with the same qualification may take a shift. No real names.

## Open points
Should the planner be able to auto-approve swaps between two qualified staff?
