# Fabrikam Field — sample brief

The kit's own example, hand-written: what a factory run's brief looks like when it reaches the demo
builder. Every name here is invented.

## The product

Fabrikam Field plans and tracks maintenance jobs for a team of field technicians: heating, cooling,
lifts and electrical work in customers' buildings.

## Who it's for

**A dispatcher** at a maintenance company with 6–10 technicians. 07:40, the start of the day: jobs
left over from earlier in the week, two technicians on call, and a customer already phoning about a lift.

## The moment the demo shows

1. Open on **Today**: how many jobs, how many late, who is free.
2. Spot the urgent lift job and open it.
3. Go to the **Schedule** and see where it fits.
4. Create a **new job** for the caller.
5. Move a job to *Done* on the **Board**.

## Screens

- Today: four numbers, the week's completed jobs, today's jobs.
- Schedule: technicians × hours, today's jobs as blocks, the current time.
- Jobs: every job, searchable, filtered by status and priority; a job opens in a drawer; "New job".
- Board: the jobs by status; "Move to".

## Look

The sample kit: warm and practical, Figtree for text, Bricolage Grotesque for headings, a green
primary. Dense enough for an office screen, comfortable on a tablet.
