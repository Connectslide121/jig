# AI feature UX

Use when creating or substantially changing a user-facing AI interaction, generated output or
AI-assisted decision. Apply proportionate product design; this is not a compliance certification.

- Identify what is AI-generated, who uses it, what data it receives and the consequence of an
  incorrect answer/action. Show the user enough context to understand the feature's role.
- Include a concise, accessible notice near the AI interaction or output, in the app's language.
  Examples: "AI assistant · AI can make mistakes" and "AI-generated summary · Check important details".
  Reuse an existing suitable disclosure, reserve its space, and avoid repeated banners. This is
  product UI guidance, not a disclaimer appended to every assistant chat reply.
- Distinguish generated drafts/suggestions from verified facts or completed actions. Show sources
  when available and relevant; never fabricate citations, confidence percentages or certainty.
- Keep users able to inspect/edit a draft before consequential publication or external action,
  cancel long work, retry a failure and recover from an incorrect result where practical.
- Make personal/sensitive data handling visible when the user needs to decide whether to proceed.
  Minimize data sent to providers and recorded in logs; do not imply private/offline processing
  unless the implementation actually guarantees it.
- Handle refusal, missing evidence, unavailable providers and partial results honestly. Preserve
  user input. Do not silently substitute invented answers for tool failures.
- For consequential health, employment, credit, education or other sensitive decisions, identify
  the need for qualified human review and a feature-specific assessment before claiming readiness.
- When a legal assessment is needed, consult current official EU guidance and the actual use case.
  Direct AI interaction disclosures, synthetic-content marking/labelling, roles and exceptions
  vary. "AI can make mistakes" alone does not establish compliance. Do not add unsupported
  "EU AI Act compliant" badges, legal claims or a generic mandatory compliance gate.

Official transparency guidance:
https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/guidelines-ai-transparency-obligations
Article 50: https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-50
