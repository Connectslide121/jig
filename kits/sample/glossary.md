# Sample glossary

Words a demo built on the sample kit uses consistently. A run's own kit replaces this with the
customer's terms (from the interview), so screens, documents and the story say the same thing.

| Term | Means | Not |
|---|---|---|
| Job | One piece of work for a customer, with a time and a person | ticket, task, order |
| Technician | The person who does the job on site | engineer, worker |
| Dispatcher | The person who plans and assigns jobs | planner, admin |
| Customer | The organisation the job is for | client, account |
| Late | Not started at its planned time | overdue, delayed |
