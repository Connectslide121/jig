# Sample brand

The kit's neutral default, used when a run has no brand of its own.

- **Feel:** calm, competent, quiet. A tool people use all day, not a marketing page.
- **Colour:** a deep blue primary for actions and the current place; neutrals for everything else.
  Status colours (success, warning, danger, info) only for status. Dark mode mirrors light.
- **Type:** one sans family (`typeface.body`), tabular numbers in tables and KPIs. Headings are
  short and sentence case.
- **Shape:** medium radius (`radius.base`), soft elevation on cards and overlays only.
- **Voice:** plain and direct. Say what happened and what to do next. No exclamation marks, no
  jargon the persona would not use.
